## What This Is

Not a newsletter. Not a content strategy. A place to write things down when they feel worth writing and to keep a public record of what I'm reading, watching, and listening to.

The writing covers systems, markets, human behaviour, and occasionally whatever is bothering me about how the world works.

---

## Posts

**The Future Is a Trade** — A framework for thinking about life as a portfolio of directional bets. How to reason about trade-offs, positioning, and long-term conviction the same way you'd think about a trade.

**The Thermodynamics of Friendship** — Why friendships fade, stabilize, or compound — framed through the lens of energy, entropy, and effort. What the physics metaphor actually reveals about human relationships that straightforward psychology misses.

## Site Design

Plain HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. No framework, no build step, no dependencies to maintain.

The layout is a three-column grid: curated bookmarks on both sides (music, books, films, articles), posts in the centre. The sidebar links are personal — the actual albums, books, and films, not a "content" version of them.

A custom music player is embedded and ships with three tracks. PJAX handles page transitions without full reloads. Post popups open inline without navigating away.

The aesthetic is deliberate: monospace font, neon green on black, thick borders. Not trying to look like a SaaS product.
