All relationships in life are investments

Surround yourself with people who share your values and your vision for the future. Life is too short to invest time and energy in people who drain it and give no return.

I will attempt to explain why this way of thinking is not transactional, but rooted in reality.

It can be said that one should not live life thinking they are “investing” in other people for returns because it sounds transactional, but it’s absolutely true. You ARE investing time, energy, and money when you choose to form long-term bonds with people; you DO seek returns on those investments. Partner? Friend? Rival? All of these require effort, and when you put in the effort, you expect effort in return because forming a long-term relationship is a two-way street. In personal life, and in business.

As the saying goes, play long-term games with long-term people. The whole “if your friend circle is not discussing ***insert some scam*** you are in the wrong friends circle” meme is not applicable here because I’m not saying people you hang out with should only discuss “serious” things or don’t have disagreements, I just mean to say if they’re not pushing you to grow in ways YOU want to grow, it’s -ev. In the long run, people you hang out with, especially in your teens and in your 20’s, impact your life trajectory A LOT.

First Law of Thermodynamics: Energy cannot be created or destroyed — only transferred or converted.

Every relationship is an energy exchange.
